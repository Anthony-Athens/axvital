# Linked observational factors — Sprint 3

Observational experiments now link existing check-ins, diet assessments, food/beverage/ingredient evidence and supplement use to their outcome dates. Nothing creates a second consumption log, protocol-adherence checkbox or timeline entry. Existing intervention authoring, billing/paywall rules and immutable outcome definitions remain in place. Synthetic examples are confined to disposable test databases.

## Supported factors

| Factor | Source and measurement | Determination and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weight, energy, mood, sleep quality | Existing Sprint 1 daily check-ins; recorded measurement | Missing is distinct from zero. Weight keeps verified kg normalization and the existing optional seven-calendar-day average. Dated factor configurations determine each plotted date's alignment. |
| Diet enrollment | `readDietAssessment(client, enrollmentId, localDate)` | Adherent, Nonadherent, Needs review, Incomplete and Planned exception remain distinct. Includes enrollment, applicable immutable rule-version ID, completeness, reasons and fingerprint. Outside/cancelled enrollment dates are Not applicable. Retrieval failure is Error. |
| Canonical food or category | Existing canonical identity and logged/library composite leaves | Presence only; quantities are not inferred. Known reliable exposure wins even with partial logging. Absence requires confirmed complete intake and sufficiently resolved identities/categories. Unknown category information is retained. |
| Ingredient classification | Existing reviewed shared/private evidence: dairy, gluten, meat, fish, egg, animal/plant-derived, grain, legume, added sugar and alcohol | Present/Absent/Unknown. No macro-based ingredient inference. Unreviewed catalog data and facts about an unconfirmed AI identity cannot establish an exposure. Explicit reviews of actual logged items/components can. These are ingredient facts, never allergy/celiac safety certifications. |
| Alcohol | Reviewed alcohol classification and reliable positive ethanol nutrient snapshots | Presence is separate from total quantity. Complete, resolved canonical intake with known ethanol snapshots can supply grams of ethanol. Legacy drinks without a quantified ethanol field keep quantity unknown. No glass-to-standard-drink assumption, ABV estimation, standard-drink calculation or hours-before-sleep measure. Only positively identified alcohol sources contribute to latest logged time. Legacy clock times are displayed in the source zone; ambiguous repeated-hour ordering is left unavailable. |
| Supplement product/formulation | Existing supplement health-event logs, explicitly assigned to a private immutable product identity | Recorded use, compatible logged dose, clock time; no confirmed use only after supplement-domain completeness confirmation and resolved product identities. Missing/unreviewed logs remain Unknown. Different products/formulations never merge automatically. |

Supplement identities were a missing prerequisite: the repository previously held only free-text names, dose/unit and clock time. The new Supplements review workflow explicitly assigns those existing events to a user-owned product/formulation. New manual/voice logs continue through the existing ingestion workflow and remain unreviewed until assigned. No supplement schedule or recommendation is created. An exact duplicate product/formulation definition is rejected. Formulation changes require a new identity.

Doses normalize grams/milligrams/micrograms to mg (1 g = 1000 mg; 1 mcg = 0.001 mg). Capsule/tablet/IU/mL totals require the same reviewed product and compatible identical unit family. A capsule is never converted to mg or combined with tablets. Missing/nonpositive doses, unsupported units, mixed unit families and incomplete logging leave the total unknown; individual recorded doses remain visible in evidence references. Amount comparisons require a known total, separately from use/presence comparisons.

## Creating an experiment yourself

1. Continue normal manual/voice intake and supplement logging. Use **Nutrition → Review intake** to review identities/classifications (including dairy or gluten) and confirm food/drink completeness. This works without a diet enrollment. Explicit zero intake requires its own acknowledgment. The review controls reuse Sprint 2 private evidence and corrections.
2. Use **Nutrition → Diets** to create/follow a diet if you want a diet factor. Use **Nutrition → Supplements** to create precise product/formulation identities, assign existing supplement logs, and confirm supplement logging for a chosen source date/timezone. Supplement confirmation is independent of food completeness, and an empty list requires explicitly confirming deliberate no use.
3. Open **Experiments → Create observational experiment**. Select/create a reusable custom outcome and configure the question, dates and timezone. The observed date is the period described, not the submission date.
4. In Linked factors choose a type, an applicable enrollment/food/category/classification/product, and same-day or preceding-day timing; Add linked factor. Each row names its source and supported measurement. For a Tuesday morning submission about Monday night, choose Monday as the outcome date: same-day factors read Monday, preceding-day factors read Sunday. If Tuesday itself is the outcome date, preceding-day factors read Monday. No additional lookback is introduced.
5. Save draft, then Save and start. In daily entry review Automatically linked factors and actual source dates. Source editing/review links open another tab so an unsaved outcome remains in place. Return and Refresh linked evidence; source confirmations belong to Nutrition or Supplements. Source failures clear stale evidence and display Error rather than absence. Record the outcome once using its existing shared metric/date observation.
6. To change factors/alignment after starting, expand Edit factors with an effective date. Historical changes require acknowledging the affected scope. Save a dated configuration; earlier dates retain their configuration until explicitly corrected. A later effective version ends the preceding version's interval. Latest revision wins on equal effective dates. Duplicate equivalent identities are rejected even if their timing differs; ingredient alcohol and Alcohol exposure are equivalent for this purpose.
7. Date-aligned factor history shows up to 31 elapsed dates ending on the selected date, with outcome, factor/source dates, completeness, uncertainty, configuration and pair eligibility. Select earlier dates for earlier windows. Counts are scoped to the displayed window, not presented as whole-study counts.

## Evidence, corrections and freshness

The additive source-reader extraction reuses Sprint 2's canonical snapshot and fingerprint unchanged, and Diets continues to call its existing evaluator. Food exposure calls the existing `intakeLeaves`; it never examines diet allow/exclude rules or exceptions. Private evidence specificity remains logged component/item, logged anchor/event, user food, explicitly reusable canonical-food correction, then reviewed shared evidence. A serving-specific correction does not classify another serving. Reusable corrections are explicitly acknowledged by the user and can recalculate historical matching intake. Diet permissions never become global food facts.

The intake anchor uniquely belongs to one canonical nutrition entry or legacy health event. Composite leaves replace parent/item representations for ingredient/category and quantity evaluation; recorded use of a named canonical dish also remains a food identity without adding parent quantities; overlapping derived evidence references are deduplicated. Current voice/manual ingestion already converges to one canonical intake representation. Separate legacy and canonical records without an explicit shared identity remain separate source records; the adapter does not guess duplicates by similar text or times.

Derived factor evidence is read on demand; nothing persists a second assessment cache. A request reuses source-day promises locally to avoid duplicate reads, then discards them. Food completeness uses the existing nutrition day/fingerprint and Diets invalidation triggers. Supplement confirmation uses its own day/timezone/fingerprint, under the same owner lock as ingestion; supplement creation/edit/delete/type/date/identity changes invalidate old and new dates. Ingredient/library/catalog changes affect the current intake fingerprint. Diet rule/schedule changes select the applicable rule version afresh. UI refresh/focus rereads sources, clears previous derived values during recalculation, and does not overwrite unsaved outcome input. There is no realtime subscription: use focus or Refresh after another tab changes data.

Continuation review confirmed the existing implementation and corrected request-local diet evidence reuse: when a dated configuration switches from same-day to preceding-day alignment and two outcome dates share a source date, each returned factor retains its own configured offset. A real PostgreSQL adapter regression covers this boundary.

Each individual database snapshot is coherent. Different factor domains are separate reads; there is no claim of one cross-domain transaction snapshot. Diet source dates use the selected enrollment's timezone, explicitly shown in the picker and evidence. Other factors use the experiment timezone; legacy date-only events retain their literal logged date. Day alignment is calendar arithmetic across DST; no values carry across gaps. Clock-only supplement/legacy beverage times cannot support reliable sleep-relative durations.

## Usable pairs and Sprint 4 interface

`readLinkedEvidence(client, userId, study, outcomeDate, windowStart, windowEnd)` in `lib/experiments/linked-service.ts` returns `LinkedResponse`: immutable factor versions, daily evidence and date-aligned history. An owner-authenticated client is required; API callers first authorize the study. Window size is at most 31 days, includes the selected outcome date, and is clipped to elapsed study dates. Metadata/source caps fail closed rather than silently truncate or claim absence.

`Evidence` in `lib/experiments/linked-evidence.ts` is contract version 1 / calculation version `linked-v1`. It includes normalized factor identity/configuration version, outcome date, resolved source date/timezone (one calendar-day period), status/value/units, nullable total amount/dose and units, completeness, uncertainty explanation, source references, source fingerprint/current-or-error freshness, latest available logged time, and the full Diets assessment where applicable.

`usablePair(evidence, outcome, measurement = 'status')` explains eligibility. A valid recorded outcome (including zero) pairs with a recorded check-in or resolved Present/Absent/Adherent/Nonadherent/Planned exception category. Known positive exposure/nonadherence can be usable despite otherwise incomplete logs. Planned exception is its own category. Unknown, Needs review, Incomplete, Missing, Not applicable and Error are excluded; missing/not-observed outcomes are excluded separately. `'amount'` additionally requires a known total; an unknown dose never becomes zero. Future comparisons must group compatible measurement units and distinguish configuration/rule versions and exceptions. No trigger ranking, hypothesis tests, multivariable modeling, causal interpretation or recommendations are implemented.

## Migration and validation

Apply all migrations in filename order through Sprint 1 `202610080004_observational_experiments.sql`, Sprint 2 `202610080005_diets.sql`, then **`202610080006_linked_factors.sql`**. The new transaction adds three owner tables: `supplement_products`, `supplement_log_days`, `observational_factor_versions`; an ownership-checked product reference on existing supplement health events; guarded source/completeness/review/configuration RPCs; and invalidation triggers. Account export/schema validation and auth-user cascades include the new records. The original observational save RPC's factor-validation block is replaced with a guarded patch; paywall, outcome, observation and lifecycle behavior are preserved, and the migration fails if the expected prerequisite definition is absent.

First apply to disposable local/staging with the existing AXVital schema (for a configured local Supabase project: `supabase migration up --local`). Run tests/typecheck/lint/build, then isolated owner/cross-owner review, stale confirmation, export and deletion checks. No production migration or deployment was performed. No real account data, diet enrollment, personal experiment or supplement choices were created.
